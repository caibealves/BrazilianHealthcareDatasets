# Brazilian Healthcare Datasets

Here are available the workflow to get the information from the Brazilian healthcare system (DATASUS).

We downloaded and processed deaths from different causes (suicide, accidents, homicides...) from the Mortality Information System (SIM).

We downloaded and processed interpersonal violences foucusing on suicide attempts from the Notifiable Diseases Information System (SINAN).

We downloaded and processed information from the healthcare infrastructure in Brazil from the National Register of Health Establishments (CNES).
